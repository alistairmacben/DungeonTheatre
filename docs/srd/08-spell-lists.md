# Spell lists by class (p105–113)

Transcribed verbatim from the SRD. These are the **class → level → spell name**
associations, which is a separate dataset from the spell descriptions
themselves (`08b-spell-descriptions.md`). Every spell appears on at least one
list; a spell's presence on multiple lists is what makes multiclass spell
attribution matter (`03-progression.md`).

**Seven lists.** Only seven classes have spell lists in the SRD: bard, cleric,
druid, paladin, ranger, sorcerer, warlock, wizard — eight, in fact. **Paladin
and ranger have no cantrips** (half-casters start at 1st level and the SRD
grants them no cantrip list), and **paladin's list stops at 5th level**,
**ranger's at 5th level** — correct, since half-casters never exceed 5th-level
slots. **Warlock's list runs to 9th** even though pact magic caps at 5th-level
slots, because Mystic Arcanum grants 6th–9th.

## Observations for the data model

1. **The class↔spell relation is many-to-many and must be its own table.**
   *Dispel Magic* is on five lists; *Eldritch Blast* on one. A spell's level is
   a property of the spell, not of the list — no spell appears at different
   levels on different lists in the SRD.

2. **The subclass expanded lists are absent.** The SRD's single subclass per
   class does add domain/circle spells (e.g. Life Domain), and those are
   recorded in `02-classes.md`. They add to the *prepared/known* set without
   changing list membership.

3. **Warlock 6th–9th entries exist only for Mystic Arcanum**, which is a
   once-per-long-rest free casting, not a slot. So "has a 9th-level spell on
   the list" does not imply "has 9th-level slots" — the UI must not infer slot
   availability from list membership.

4. **Paladin and ranger have no cantrips at all** in the SRD. A cantrip section
   must therefore be conditionally absent, not empty-but-present.

5. **The SRD's list names are the canonical spell IDs.** Note the two entries
   the SRD renames from their familiar forms — the SRD strips product-identity
   names, so e.g. *Tiny Hut*, *Magnificent Mansion*, *Arcane Hand*,
   *Arcane Sword*, *Floating Disk*, *Faithful Hound*, *Private Sanctum*,
   *Secret Chest*, *Instant Summons*, *Resilient Sphere*, *Freezing Sphere*,
   *Irresistible Dance*, *Magic Aura* appear without the "Leomund's" /
   "Mordenkainen's" / "Otiluke's" / "Bigby's" / "Tenser's" / "Nystul's"
   prefixes. **Use the SRD names verbatim** — inventing the trademarked
   prefixes would break the CC-BY licence position.
