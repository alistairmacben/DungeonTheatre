# Equipment (p62–74)

## Armour

**Proficiency penalty** — wearing armour you lack proficiency with gives
**disadvantage on every ability check, save and attack involving STR or DEX**,
and **you cannot cast spells**.

**Dex contribution by category:**
- **Light** — add full DEX modifier
- **Medium** — add DEX modifier, **max +2**
- **Heavy** — **no DEX at all**, but a negative DEX modifier is *not* applied
  either
- **Shield** — flat **+2**, only one at a time, carried in one hand

**Strength requirement** — if the armour lists Str 13 or Str 15 and your
Strength score is lower, **speed −10 ft**. (Not a proficiency gate; a speed
penalty. Dwarves ignore it, see races.)

**Stealth** — flagged armours give **disadvantage on DEX (Stealth)**.

## Weapon proficiency

Categories: **simple** and **martial**. Proficiency adds the proficiency bonus
to the **attack roll** only. Without proficiency you simply don't add it —
there is no additional penalty (unlike armour).

## Weapon properties

| Property | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Ammunition** | Requires ammunition; one piece expended per attack. Drawing it is part of the attack (needs a free hand for one-handed weapons). **Recover half after the battle** with a minute's search. Used in melee it counts as improvised. |
| **Finesse** | Choose **STR or DEX** for attack *and* damage — **the same modifier for both**. |
| **Heavy** | **Small creatures have disadvantage** on attacks with it. |
| **Light** | Eligible for two-weapon fighting. |
| **Loading** | Only **one** piece of ammunition per action, bonus action or reaction, **regardless of how many attacks you have**. |
| **Range** | Two numbers (normal/long). **Disadvantage beyond normal range; cannot attack beyond long range.** |
| **Reach** | **+5 ft** to reach, including for opportunity attacks. |
| **Special** | Weapon-specific rules (Lance, Net). |
| **Thrown** | Throw for a ranged attack **using the same ability modifier as the melee attack** would use (so a thrown dagger may use DEX because it is finesse). |
| **Two-Handed** | Requires two hands to attack. |
| **Versatile** | Usable one- or two-handed; the parenthesised die is the **two-handed** damage. |

**Improvised weapons** — 1d4, GM-assigned damage type; a ranged weapon used in
melee, or a non-thrown melee weapon thrown, also deals 1d4. Improvised thrown
range 20/60. A GM may allow proficiency if the object resembles a real weapon.

**Silvered weapons** — 100 gp to silver one weapon or ten pieces of ammunition.
Matters because some creatures resist non-magical damage but not silver.

**Special weapons:**
- **Lance** — disadvantage against targets within 5 ft; requires two hands
  unless mounted.
- **Net** — a Large or smaller creature hit is **restrained**; no effect on
  formless or Huge+ creatures. Escape with a DC 10 STR check, or deal 5
  slashing to the net (AC 10). Only **one attack** when used, regardless of
  attack count.

## Adventuring gear with mechanics

Items that are effectively **DM-creatable consumables** — good validation that
the item vocabulary must cover attacks, saves, conditions and charges:

- **Acid** (vial) — ranged improvised attack within 20 ft, **2d6 acid**.
- **Alchemist's Fire** — ranged improvised attack, **1d4 fire at the start of
  each of the target's turns**; DC 10 DEX check as an action to extinguish.
- **Antitoxin** — advantage on saves against poison for 1 hour; no effect on
  undead or constructs.
- **Ball Bearings** — cover a 10 ft square; DC 10 DEX save or fall **prone**;
  no save if moving at half speed.
- **Caltrops** — 5 ft square; DC 15 DEX save or stop moving and take 1
  piercing; **speed −10 ft until healed to at least 1 HP**.
- **Healer's Kit** — **ten charges**; expend one to stabilise a creature at
  0 HP with no Medicine check.
- **Holy Water** — ranged improvised attack; **2d6 radiant** against fiends and
  undead only. Craftable: 1 hour, 25 gp of powdered silver, and a 1st-level
  spell slot.
- **Hunting Trap** — DC 13 DEX save or 1d4 piercing and movement restricted;
  DC 13 STR check to escape, 1 piercing per failure.
- **Crowbar** — **advantage on STR checks where leverage applies** *(another
  narrative condition — needs a player-facing toggle)*.
- **Climber's Kit**, **Block and Tackle**, **Chain** (10 HP, DC 20 STR to
  burst), **Candle** (light radii).
- **Spellcasting foci** — **Arcane focus** (sorcerer, warlock, wizard),
  **Druidic focus** (druid), **Holy symbol** (cleric, paladin; must be held,
  worn visibly, or borne on a shield), **Component pouch** (covers all material
  components **except those with a listed cost**).

### Light sources (a derived "vision" concern, not a stat)

| Source | Bright | Dim (additional) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Candle | 5 ft | — | 1 hour |
| Torch | 20 ft | 20 ft | 1 hour |
| Lamp | 15 ft | 30 ft | 6 hr / pint oil |
| Lantern, hooded | 30 ft | 30 ft | 6 hr / pint oil |
| Lantern, bullseye | 60 ft **cone** | 60 ft | 6 hr / pint oil |

Hooded lantern can be lowered as an **action** to dim light in a 5 ft radius.
Light is emitted by a *carried item*, has a **shape** (radius or cone), two
**tiers** (bright / dim) and a **fuel clock**. Cross-reference with the Light
cantrip and Darkvision from races — these must all feed one lighting model.

### Remaining mechanical gear (p68)

- **Lock** — DC 15 DEX check with thieves' tools to pick. Better locks at GM
  discretion (so **DC is an item property, not a constant**).
- **Manacles** — bind Small or Medium; DC 20 DEX to escape, DC 20 STR to break,
  DC 15 DEX to pick. **15 HP.**
- **Magnifying Glass** — **advantage** on ability checks to appraise or inspect
  a small or highly detailed item. Also a fire-starting substitute in sunlight.
- **Oil (flask)** — improvised ranged attack, throw up to 20 ft. On a hit the
  target is coated; if it takes **any fire damage within 1 minute** it takes an
  extra **5 fire**. Poured on the ground: 5 ft square, burns 2 rounds, **5 fire**
  to a creature entering or ending its turn there, **once per turn**.
- **Poison, Basic (vial)** — coats **one** slashing/piercing weapon or **three**
  pieces of ammunition; an action to apply; **DC 10 CON save or 1d4 poison**;
  **potency lasts 1 minute** after application.
- **Potion of Healing** — **2d4 + 2 HP**; an action to drink *or administer to
  another*.
- **Portable Ram** — **+4 bonus** on the STR check to break a door; a second
  character helping grants **advantage** (so "help" is a stackable, distinct
  modifier from the flat bonus).
- **Rope** — **2 HP**, burst with DC 17 STR.
- **Pouch** — 20 sling bullets or 50 blowgun needles.
- **Quiver** — 20 arrows.
- **Spellbook** — 100 blank vellum pages (a **capacity** the wizard's scribing
  rules consume).
- **Spyglass** — ×2 magnification. **Tinderbox** — action to light a torch,
  1 minute for any other fire.

*Design note:* almost every one of these is expressible as
`{ save | check, ability, DC, on-fail effect }`, `{ HP, break-DC }`,
`{ advantage-tag }`, or `{ light emission }`. Nothing here needs bespoke code —
which is the validation the DM-item design needed.

## Containers and encumbrance plumbing (p69–70)

Every container has a **capacity** in cubic feet *and/or* pounds, or in liquid
volume. Backpack 1 cu ft / 30 lb · Sack 1 cu ft / 30 lb · Chest 12 cu ft /
300 lb · Basket 2 cu ft / 40 lb · Pouch ⅕ cu ft / 6 lb · Barrel 40 gal ·
Bucket 3 gal · Waterskin 4 pints · Flask/tankard 1 pint · Jug 1 gal ·
Iron pot 1 gal · Bottle 1½ pints · Vial 4 oz.

Items may also be **strapped to the outside** of a backpack (bedroll, rope) —
i.e. the container model needs an "attached, not contained" slot, or capacity
must simply be advisory.

**Full gear table** is in the SRD at p69 (cost + weight for ~90 items). Weight
matters only through carrying capacity (see `06-core-rules.md`, STR × 15).

## Tools (p70–71)

**[IMPORTANT] Tool proficiency is not tied to an ability.** "Tool use is not
tied to a single ability, since proficiency with a tool represents broader
knowledge of its use." The GM may call for a DEX check to carve fine detail or
a STR check for hard wood — **with the same tool**.

This means an ability check has **three independent inputs**:
`ability` × `skill proficiency?` × `tool proficiency?`
and the proficiency bonus applies if *either* proficiency is relevant.
A check request therefore cannot be typed as `Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)`
alone — it needs an optional **tool** dimension.

**Each type within a group is a separate proficiency** — each artisan's tool
set, each gaming set, each musical instrument.

**Tool list** (cost / weight): Artisan's tools — alchemist's supplies 50 gp,
brewer's 20 gp, calligrapher's 10 gp, carpenter's 8 gp, cartographer's 15 gp,
cobbler's 5 gp, cook's utensils 1 gp, glassblower's 30 gp, jeweler's 25 gp,
leatherworker's 5 gp, mason's 10 gp, painter's 10 gp, potter's 10 gp, smith's
20 gp, tinker's 50 gp, weaver's 1 gp, woodcarver's 1 gp. Disguise kit 25 gp ·
Forgery kit 15 gp · Gaming set (dice 1 sp, playing cards 5 sp) · Herbalism kit
5 gp · Musical instruments (bagpipes 30, drum 6, dulcimer 25, flute 2, lute 35,
lyre 30, horn 3, pan flute 12, shawm 2, viol 30) · Navigator's tools 25 gp ·
Poisoner's kit 50 gp · Thieves' tools 25 gp · Vehicles (land/water).

**Tools with rules beyond "add proficiency":**
- **Herbalism kit** — proficiency is **required** to create antitoxin and
  potions of healing (a *gate*, not a bonus).
- **Musical instrument** — a **bard's spellcasting focus**.
- **Navigator's tools** — proficiency adds to checks to **avoid getting lost at
  sea**; also enables charting a course.
- **Thieves' tools** — adds to checks to **disarm traps or open locks**.
- **Disguise / forgery / poisoner's kits, gaming sets** — scoped proficiency
  bonus on their own activity.

*Pattern:* tool proficiency is `proficiency-bonus applies when
{ activity-tag } matches` — the same **tag-scoped** mechanism advantage needs.

- **Pulling a vehicle** = up to **5× base carrying capacity** including the
  vehicle's weight; **multiple animals add their capacities together**.
- **Barding** — any armour on the Armor table, **×4 cost, ×2 weight**. (So
  armour is a template applied to a *creature size class*, not a human-only
  item.)
- **Military saddle** — **advantage on checks to remain mounted**.
  **Exotic saddle** — *required* for aquatic or flying mounts (a gate).
- **Vehicle proficiency** — adds proficiency bonus to control checks in
  difficult circumstances.
- **Rowed vessels** — +3 mph downstream current; cannot be rowed against a
  significant current. Rowboat weighs 100 lb.

Tack: bit and bridle 2 gp · carriage 100 gp · cart 15 gp · chariot 250 gp ·
feed 5 cp/day · saddles exotic 60 / military 20 / pack 5 / riding 10 gp ·
saddlebags 4 gp · sled 20 gp · stabling 5 sp/day · wagon 35 gp.
Waterborne: galley 30,000 gp @ 4 mph · keelboat 3,000 @ 1 · longship 10,000 @ 3
· rowboat 50 @ 1½ · sailing ship 10,000 @ 2 · warship 25,000 @ 2½.

*Scope note:* mounts are only mechanically interesting through carrying
capacity and the mounted-combat rules. With **combat out of scope**, mounts
reduce to inventory + speed + a saddle gate. Model them as **items with a speed
and a capacity**, not as creatures.

## Trade goods, lifestyle, services (p72–74)

**Trade goods** hold **full resale value** (unlike gear at half): wheat 1 cp/lb
· flour or chicken 2 cp · salt 5 cp · iron or canvas 1 sp · copper or cotton
5 sp · ginger or goat 1 gp · cinnamon/pepper or sheep 2 gp · cloves or pig
3 gp · silver or linen 5 gp · silk or cow 10 gp · saffron or ox 15 gp · gold
50 gp/lb · platinum 500 gp/lb.

**Lifestyle expenses** (per **day**, chosen per week or month):
Wretched — free · Squalid 1 sp · Poor 2 sp · Modest 1 gp · Comfortable 2 gp ·
Wealthy 4 gp · Aristocratic 10 gp minimum.
**No mechanical effect in the SRD** — consequences are explicitly narrative
("might help you make contacts", "run the risk of attracting thieves").

**Self-sufficiency** — living off the land costs no coin but consumes downtime.
Practising a profession = **Poor** lifestyle; **proficiency in Survival** =
**Comfortable** lifestyle. *(A rare case of a skill proficiency having a direct
non-check effect.)*

**Food, drink, lodging** — ale gallon 2 sp / mug 4 cp · banquet 10 gp/person ·
bread 2 cp · cheese 1 sp · meat 3 sp · wine common 2 sp / fine 10 gp.
Inn per day: squalid 7 cp, poor 1 sp, modest 5 sp, comfortable 8 sp, wealthy
2 gp, aristocratic 4 gp. Meals per day: squalid 3 cp, poor 6 cp, modest 3 sp,
comfortable 5 sp, wealthy 8 sp, aristocratic 2 gp.

**Services** — coach cab 3 cp/mile between towns, 1 cp within a city ·
hireling skilled 2 gp/day, untrained 2 sp/day · messenger 2 cp/mile · toll
1 cp · ship's passage 1 sp/mile. "Skilled" = any hireling whose service
involves a proficiency.

**Spellcasting services** — deliberately unpriced; 1st–2nd level spells roughly
10–50 gp plus material components. Purely a GM adjudication.

*Scope decision to raise with the user:* lifestyle, services and trade goods
carry **almost no automatable mechanics**. They are worth including as
**reference data** for a shop/economy UI, but they generate no derived stats.

## Currency and trade (p62)

cp / sp / ep / gp / pp. 1 gp = 10 sp = 100 cp; 1 ep = 5 sp; 1 pp = 10 gp.
50 coins = 1 lb.

Selling: gear at **half** cost; gems, art and trade goods at **full** value.
Magic items are deliberately unpriced.
