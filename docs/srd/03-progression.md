# Progression, multiclassing, backgrounds (p56–61)

## Character advancement

XP thresholds L1–20 with proficiency bonus: **+2** at 1–4, **+3** at 5–8,
**+4** at 9–12, **+5** at 13–16, **+6** at 17–20.

**Hit points per level** — roll the class Hit Die + CON modifier, or take the
fixed average (rounded up).

**[IMPORTANT] Raising CON is retroactive.** "When your Constitution modifier
increases by 1, your hit point maximum increases by 1 for each level you have
attained." HP maximum is a *function*, not an accumulator.

**Ability Score Improvement** — +2 to one score or +1 to two; cap 20 (unless a
feature raises the cap). **ASI levels are per-class:** most classes get 4/8/12/
16/19; **Fighter** adds 6 and 14 (seven total); **Rogue** adds 10 (six total).

## Multiclassing (p57–58)

**Prerequisites** — you must meet the ability minimum for **both** the class
you are leaving and the one you are entering:

| Class | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Barbarian | STR 13 |
| Bard | CHA 13 |
| Cleric | WIS 13 |
| Druid | WIS 13 |
| Fighter | STR 13 **or** DEX 13 |
| Monk | DEX 13 **and** WIS 13 |
| Paladin | STR 13 **and** CHA 13 |
| Ranger | DEX 13 **and** WIS 13 |
| Rogue | DEX 13 |
| Sorcerer | CHA 13 |
| Warlock | CHA 13 |
| Wizard | INT 13 |

- **XP and proficiency bonus** use **total character level**, never class level.
- **Hit points** — you gain the new class's *per-level* HP. The 1st-level
  maximum HP is granted **only for your first class**.
- **Hit Dice** — pooled; tracked separately when die types differ.
- **Proficiencies** — a **reduced set** when taking a new class:

| Class | Gained on multiclassing in |
|---|---|
| Barbarian | shields, simple weapons, martial weapons |
| Bard | light armour, one skill, one musical instrument |
| Cleric | light armour, medium armour, shields |
| Druid | light armour, medium armour, shields |
| Fighter | light + medium armour, shields, simple + martial weapons |
| Monk | simple weapons, shortswords |
| Paladin | light + medium armour, shields, simple + martial weapons |
| Ranger | light + medium armour, shields, simple + martial weapons, one skill |
| Rogue | light armour, one skill, thieves' tools |
| Sorcerer | — |
| Warlock | light armour, simple weapons |
| Wizard | — |

- **You do not receive the new class's starting equipment.**

### Features with special multiclass rules

- **Channel Divinity** — effects from both classes become available, but
  **uses do not stack**; extra uses come only from explicit class levels.
- **Extra Attack** — **does not stack** across classes. Warlock's *Thirsting
  Blade* likewise gives nothing extra if you already have Extra Attack.
- **Unarmored Defense** — cannot be gained twice.
- **Spellcasting** — see below.

### Multiclass spellcasting

- **Spells known and prepared are determined per class, independently**, as if
  single-classed in each.
- **Each spell is associated with the class that granted it**, and uses **that
  class's spellcasting ability**. A spellcasting focus works only for that
  class's spells.
- **Spell slots** come from a single combined table. Caster level =
  **full levels** in bard, cleric, druid, sorcerer, wizard
  **+ half (rounded down)** levels in paladin and ranger.
  **Warlock is excluded** from this calculation.
- Higher slots may be used to cast lower-level spells, **including their
  upcast effects**, even with no spells of that level known.
- **Pact Magic interoperates both ways**: pact slots can cast Spellcasting
  spells, and Spellcasting slots can cast warlock spells.

## Alignment (p58–59)

Nine alignments. **Purely descriptive — no mechanics attached.** Do not model
as rules; store as a character label only.

---

## Languages (p59)

**Standard:** Common, Dwarvish, Elvish, Giant, Gnomish, Goblin, Halfling, Orc.
**Exotic:** Abyssal, Celestial, Draconic, Deep Speech, Infernal, Primordial,
Sylvan, Undercommon. **Secret:** thieves' cant, Druidic.
Scripts are shared (Common, Dwarvish, Elvish, Infernal, Celestial, Draconic).
Primordial has dialects (Auran, Aquan, Ignan, Terran) that are mutually
intelligible.

---

## Inspiration (p59–60)

- **Binary** — you either have it or you don't; it cannot be stockpiled.
- Spend to gain **advantage** on one attack roll, save or ability check.
- **Transferable to another player** as a reward.

A tiny resource, but a genuinely multiplayer one — a player-to-player transfer
with no DM involvement.

## Backgrounds (p60–61)

Each background grants:
- **two skill proficiencies**
- usually **tool proficiencies**
- sometimes **languages**
- an **equipment package**
- a **feature** (typically narrative, not mechanical)
- suggested personality trait / ideal / bond / flaw (roleplay only)

**Duplicate proficiency rule:** if a proficiency would be gained twice, choose a
different one **of the same kind** (skill or tool) instead. This is a general
rule and applies across all sources.

**Customising:** replace the feature, choose any two skills, and any two tool
proficiencies or languages.

**The SRD contains exactly one background: Acolyte.**
Insight + Religion, two languages, holy symbol / prayer book / incense /
vestments / common clothes / 15 gp, feature *Shelter of the Faithful*
(narrative: free healing and modest lifestyle support at temples of your faith).

## Equipment: currency and trade (p62)

**Coins:** cp, sp, ep, gp, pp. 1 gp = 10 sp = 100 cp; 1 ep = 5 sp; 1 pp = 10 gp.
50 coins weigh 1 lb.

**Selling:** weapons, armour and gear fetch **half** cost. Gems, art objects
and trade goods retain **full** value. Magic items are deliberately not
priced — "the value of magic is far beyond simple gold".

**Armour proficiency penalty** — wearing armour you lack proficiency with:
**disadvantage on every ability check, saving throw and attack roll that
involves STR or DEX, and you cannot cast spells at all.**
